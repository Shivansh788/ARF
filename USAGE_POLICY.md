# Responsible Use Policy

ARF is for authorized security testing of AI agents and MCP integrations that you own or have explicit permission to assess. Use local or sandboxed targets by default. Do not use probes to access, alter, or exfiltrate real data or to disrupt systems.

The demo adapter is intentionally local and uses fake canary data. Operators are responsible for authorization, scope, rate limits, data handling, and removing sensitive traces after testing.
